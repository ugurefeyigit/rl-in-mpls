\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

\paragraph{Software and hardware.} Python 3.13, PyTorch 2.14 (CPU build),
Stable-Baselines3 and SB3-contrib 2.9, SciPy HiGHS for MILP-track; exact
versions are pinned in \texttt{requirements/lock-cpu-py313.txt}. All post-V2
results were produced on one 4-core x86-64 CPU machine without a GPU, with one
PyTorch thread per training process and at most four concurrent training
processes. Training a learner for 400k transitions took 0.5--1\,h of wall
time on this machine.

\paragraph{Determinism.} Episode seeds are derived from the training root
(training) or listed explicitly (evaluation); traffic is generated from
per-episode generators that no routing decision consumes, so every controller
sees identical traffic on a given episode. On CPU with one thread a training
run is deterministic given its root; the study trainer reproduces the closed
study's trainer bit for bit (a slow test checks the network parameters). The
closed study's own learner runs used CUDA and are therefore reproduced
statistically, not exactly (\S\ref{sec:repro}); its baselines reproduce
exactly.

\paragraph{One-command entry points.}
\texttt{python scripts/reproduce.py --suite sanity} (tests, exact baseline
reproduction, a short training and evaluation of both learners; minutes),
\texttt{--suite core} (one further 400k root of both learners and all
reference policies; hours) and \texttt{--suite full} (every registered run;
about a day on the machine above). \texttt{--suite analysis} rebuilds every
table, every number in this paper (\texttt{paper/generated/numbers.tex}), the
figures and the PDF from the stored per-episode results.

\paragraph{Provenance.} Every experiment is declared in
\texttt{experiments/registry/*.yaml} before it runs; each run writes a
manifest with the git commit, resolved configuration, environment
specification, seeds, integrity counters and wall time. Per-episode test
results of every checkpoint that was evaluated, the mask audit and the
sequentiality diagnostic are versioned under \texttt{experiments/raw/}.
Deviations from the preregistered plan (\texttt{docs/HYPOTHESES.md}) and
failed or interrupted runs are recorded in \texttt{docs/EXPERIMENT\_LOG.md};
interrupted runs were restarted from scratch with the same root and their
partial outputs kept.
