\section{Preregistered hypotheses and outcomes}
\label{app:hypotheses}

Hypotheses H0--H8 were committed before any new learner result existed
(\texttt{docs/HYPOTHESES.md}); H9--H14 were added later, each before its
experiment ran, and are exploratory.
Three of the five primary training roots are the reproduction runs, whose
direction was known from the closed study when H1 was written; the two
additional roots were new. The frozen-exogenous rollout was added after an
early look at the clairvoyant diagnostic.
