\subsection{RQ5: When does a myopic learner fail?}
\label{sec:rq5}

In the base environment a move carries traffic in the interval in which it is
made, so its first-interval effect is in the immediate reward. The
delayed-activation variant ($L=1$) removes that property: a request is
charged when it is made and takes effect one interval later; the observation
adds the pending request of every demand (689 features), so the state stays
Markov. Two side effects are part of the variant: a demand with a pending
request cannot be moved again, which lengthens the effective lock from three
to four intervals, and a request that has become illegal at activation is
cancelled but keeps its charge (Table~\ref{tab:delay}).

The bandit fails by construction, and the ranking of the two learners
reverses. On the same two roots the bandit falls from \DelayBanditLZero{}
without delay to \DelayBandit{}, because its regression target now contains
the cost of a move but none of its benefit, and it learns to move less. PPO's
mean is nearly unchanged (\DelayPPOLZero{} $\rightarrow$ \DelayPPO), but this
average hides opposite per-root shifts, and on root 42 PPO still flips demands
at hold-down expiry under delay; it is ahead of the bandit on
\DelayPPORootsBetter{} of \DelayPPORoots{} roots (mean \DelayPPOVs).
\input{sections/results_delay_q}
The non-learning controllers degrade because they react to a state that the
delayed move no longer matches (MILP-track \DelayRefmilptrackLZero{}
$\rightarrow$ \DelayRefmilptrack, greedy \DelayRefgreedyLZero{} $\rightarrow$
\DelayRefgreedy), yet MILP-track remains better than every learner. What this
experiment establishes is therefore narrow: a learner whose target is the
decision-time reward cannot work when effects are delayed, so some form of
delayed credit assignment is needed. It does not show that a long-horizon
learner is needed---a target that sums the next $L+1$ rewards would suffice
in principle, although at this budget neither Q-learner recovers the
no-delay return---nor that RL beats optimization-based control in this
regime.
